# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary visitors are prospective collaborators, clients, and technical
peers. They should find the site interesting and memorable, discover substantial
evidence of Lorenzo's work and interests, and leave with confidence that the
presentation is thoughtful rather than generic or AI-generated.

## Product Purpose

The site is Lorenzo Germini's professional home: a place to understand his work,
experience, projects, writing, and range of interests. It should make it easy to
evaluate him as a collaborator or AI product engineer while rewarding deeper
exploration.

## Positioning

Lorenzo is an AI product engineer who connects AI-native technical execution
with product judgment, business context, and practical value. The positioning
should demonstrate fluency with AI-native work without relying on gimmicky
language or inflated claims.

## Operating Context

Visitors may arrive for a quick professional assessment, follow a shared piece
of writing, inspect technical work, or explore Lorenzo's broader interests before
contacting or collaborating with him.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The product is a public personal website with a homepage, writing surface, and
  printable CV.
- Professional evidence must remain easy to find without forcing every visitor
  through a linear resume.
- Writing may range across AI, entrepreneurship, business, biohacking, and other
  genuine interests. Its subject matter is deliberately not locked to a narrow
  editorial category.
- AI-native positioning must remain concrete and credible, never gimmicky.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the name Lorenzo Germini.
- Preserve an intelligent, direct voice grounded in real work and interests.
- The experience should feel distinctive, visually considered, and free of
  generic “AI slop.”

## Evidence on Hand

- Structured professional history, education, skills, projects, links, and
  contact details in `src/data/resume-data.tsx`.
- Public writing content and feed sources in the repository.
- Retained technical and professional terminology defined by
  `docs/spec/retained-proof.json`.
- No testimonials, client logos, performance benchmarks, or other third-party
  endorsements may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Make depth discoverable without making the first impression laborious.
2. Demonstrate AI product judgment through concrete evidence, not slogans.
3. Balance professional credibility with a broad and genuinely personal range of
   interests.
4. Reward curiosity with memorable craft while preserving clarity and access.
5. Prefer specificity and real proof over fashionable AI language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must preserve keyboard access, visible focus, semantic structure,
responsive readability, reduced-motion preferences, and a useful print
experience.
