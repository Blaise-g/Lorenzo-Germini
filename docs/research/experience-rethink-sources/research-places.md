### 1. Cuneo (GO)

**FACT:** He grew up in Cuneo and went to high school there.

- **First return, 2022–23:** he taught maths and physics at his old school, and built a Stable Diffusion + Dreambooth interior-design MVP that was killed after customer discovery.
- **Second return, 2024 onward:** Founding AI Engineer at a stealth EdTech startup (RAG with handwritten parsing, tracing and evals, led two developers), then Complaion (an agentic core, expert feedback, production evals).

**PUBLIC, landmark:**

- **Height and peaks:** the Bisalta (also called Besimauda) has its main, southern summit at 2,231 m and a northern anticima at 2,018 m. There is a cross on each summit. [Bis]
- **Name and shape:** "Bis alta" means "twice high". The profile is described as trapezoidal and bifid, and English alpinists coined the name in the 19th century. [Bis][MTV]
- **Why it reads so clearly:** it drops straight onto the plain from over 2,000 m with no pre-Alpine band in between. It "stands out so distinctly from the plains", and Boves sits "at the foot of the Bisalta". [MTV][VC]
- **Cuneo Città alpina 2024:** Cuneo presented the Besimauda as its home mountain for the year. [MTV, snippet only]
- **Direction from the city:** no source states it. From the summit coordinates (44°16′N 7°36′E, [Bis]) it is roughly south-south-east of the centre, about 13 km away. That is derived by me, so check it against a photo.
- **The city itself:** Cuneo sits on a wedge-shaped plateau where the Stura di Demonte and Gesso rivers meet ("cuneo" means wedge). Via Roma runs from the tip of the wedge to Piazza Galimberti. [Brit]

**PUBLIC, work vocabulary:**

- **ISO certification:** a Stage 1 then Stage 2 audit, a certificate valid for 3 years, annual surveillance audits, then recertification. Findings are classed as minor or major nonconformities and need a corrective-action plan. Clauses 4–10 follow Plan-Do-Check-Act. [ISO]
- **Complaion:** founded in 2024; Italy is the second-largest country by number of ISO certificates; the company targets SMEs that need ISO for tenders. [Cx] Its own claims (€13.5M raised, "80% automation") are company PR. Do not use them as discovery numbers.
- **Handwritten parsing:** detected regions are drawn as coloured bounding boxes over the page and linked to the extracted text. [Parse]

**PROPOSALS:**

- **A. "The wedge and the two horns" (recommended).**
  - Idea: the GO tile is the wedge plateau between two river ribbons, with the bifid Bisalta behind it at the correct bearing.
  - Two documented returns become two lit buildings on the same wedge, each tagged with its dates. A school with a chalkboard and a dimmed "MVP" window labelled "killed after customer discovery" stands for 2022–23. An office stands for 2024 to now.
  - Rests on all the Cuneo FACTs. The two-peaks-equals-two-returns reading is my interpretation and must not be presented as his.
  - Inspirations: [TS] floating island plinths and [MV] isometric clarity.
  - Discovery: each time the board passes GO, the next return lights up, showing documented dates and roles only.
- **B. "Audit path up the Bisalta."**
  - Idea: a switchback trail with waymarks for Stage 1, Stage 2, surveillance and recert ([ISO], generic). Small agent figures walk it, and at each waymark an "expert" figure hands back a note, showing agents combining business context with expert feedback.
  - Discovery: an eval gauge at the summit cross reads "reliability tracked in production". It shows no number, because he has given none.
  - Needs from Lorenzo: any shareable metric, or confirmation that none should be shown.
- **C. "Handwriting on the plain."**
  - Idea: a notebook page laid flat like a field at the foot of the mountain, with bounding-box overlays lighting up as it is parsed and two small figures at the edge for the two developers he led.
  - Rests on the startup FACT. Make the page content generic: the startup is in stealth, so show no real material.

**The "?" Chance space (PROPOSAL):** an unprinted Chance card.

- Flipped, it shows the Ghigliottina format with five blank clue slots and no word.
- Or it shows the germinai seed with the book still closed.
- Either way the card says "not yet written" and links to the newsletter or contact. It invents nothing.

### 2. Turin

**FACT:** BSc in Electrical Engineering at Politecnico di Torino, 2017–2020.

**PUBLIC, landmark** (all [Mole] unless marked):

- **Height and material:** 167.5 m, and described as the tallest unreinforced brick building in the world.
- **Architect and dates:** Alessandro Antonelli, 1863–1889.
- **Silhouette:** a four-faced dome, then a tempietto, then a lantern, then a needle spire topped by a star. The [MNC] page gives the stages: Baveno granite columns in the first ~30 m, the dome at roughly 40–80 m, the tempietto above it, an 18 m lantern, then the spire with its twelve-pointed star.
- **Spire history:** a 1953 storm destroyed the top 47 m. It was rebuilt in 1961 as a metal structure faced with stone.
- **Uses:** National Museum of Cinema since 2000. A glass panoramic lift climbs an open shaft to the tempietto terrace at 85 m. [MNC]
- **Merz neon:** Mario Merz's red-neon Fibonacci numbers, _Il volo dei numeri_, sit on the dome's curve and are now lit all year. [Merz]
- **Exterior colour of the dome:** I found no source for it. Check photos before modelling.

**PUBLIC, electrical-engineering motif:**

- Galileo Ferraris, professor at the Italian Industrial Museum in Turin (which became the Politecnico), conceived the rotating magnetic field from 1885. He used two stationary perpendicular coils fed with AC currents 90° out of phase. [Ferr]
- The IEEE dedicated a Milestone at the Politecnico in 2021. No original Ferraris motor survives. [Ferr]

**PROPOSALS:**

- **A. "Ferraris' Mole" (recommended).**
  - Idea: two perpendicular coils wrap the base of the dome. The spire becomes a rotor needle.
  - Discovery: a phase slider. At 0° the needle only trembles; at 90° it spins, and the label reveals "BSc Electrical Engineering, Politecnico di Torino, 2017–2020".
  - Interpretation: this is Turin's EE heritage, not a claim that he worked on Ferraris.
  - Inspiration: [MV] for mechanisms that respond to interaction.
- **B. "Numbers in flight as a circuit."**
  - Idea: Merz's neon numbers run up the dome ribs as a lit trace and terminate at the star.
  - Discovery: hover to light the trace. Use only the Fibonacci sequence and his years, never other numbers.
  - Inspiration: [Merz].
- **C. "The lift as a scope trace."**
  - Idea: the glass lift rises through the open shaft, drawing a sine wave on its glass.
  - Risk: weaker legibility at small size.

### 3. Lausanne and Switzerland

**FACT:**

- MSc in Life Sciences Engineering (Neuroscience and Neuroengineering) at EPFL, 2020–22.
- Roche, Basel, summer 2021: a personalized-medicine filling prototype tested through hardware-software integration, plus a gradient-boosted model that tuned fill parameters 20% faster than manual tuning.
- Burgeon Labs, Geneva, Feb–Oct 2022: fine-tuned language models for abstractive summarisation of biomedical literature.
- MSc thesis: a biomedical paper summariser with "detailed analysis or TL;DR" modes.

**PUBLIC, landmark:**

- **Straight across the lake:** the Dent d'Oche (2,221 m) rises above Évian on the French shore. [Oche] Turner drew "The Lake of Geneva with the Dent d'Oche, from above Lausanne" in 1841. [Turner]
- **Up-lake to the east-south-east:** the serrated Dents du Midi (Haute Cime 3,257 m), prominent from the lake and from Montreux. [DdM] Cardinaux's Simplon Line poster shows the lake, Chillon and the Dents du Midi. [Card]
- **What tourism sources say:** "the lake and the mountains" from the cathedral esplanade, and Mont Blanc on clear days from the tower. [LT]
- **What is not confirmed:** no source names the peaks seen from Ouchy. Verify with PeakFinder.
- **EPFL:** the Rolex Learning Center by SANAA (2010) is an undulating single-storey slab. [RLC]
- **Blue Brain Project:** ran at EPFL from 2005 to December 2024 and modelled a rat neocortical column of ~10,000 neurons. [BBP] This is campus context only, not his work.
- **Geneva:** the Jet d'Eau reaches 140 m. [Jet]

**PUBLIC, work vocabulary:**

- A generic aseptic fill line: robot-loaded vial nests, a filling and stoppering isolator, a capper. [Fill]
- Note that his FACT says "filling prototype", not vials.
- Abstractive summarisation generates new sentences rather than extracting existing ones. [Parse-adjacent source: llamaindex/docsumo results]

**PROPOSALS:**

- **A. "The lake is the TL;DR" (recommended).**
  - Idea: Turner's viewpoint from above Lausanne. The far shore and mountains are the full paper, drawn as dense ridgelines of text; their reflection in the lake is the summary.
  - Discovery: a "Detailed / TL;DR" toggle redraws the reflection, mirroring his thesis's two modes. A Jet d'Eau plume at the Geneva end is tagged "Burgeon Labs, 2022".
  - Inspirations: [Turner] and [Card].
- **B. "Boosted trees on the shore."**
  - Idea: a small filling-prototype bench beside a grove of stacked little trees (gradient-boosted trees, a visual pun).
  - Discovery: a dial labelled "tuned 20% faster than manual tuning".
  - Placement: Basel is on the Rhine, not the lake, so show Roche as a jetty labelled "Basel" rather than a lakeside building.
  - Needs from Lorenzo: what the prototype filled and what it looked like.
- **C. "Learning Center wave."**
  - Idea: the undulating RLC roof read as a neural signal trace, for the neuroscience degree.

### 4. Lille

**FACT:** AI Engineer at GSK, Saint-Amand-les-Eaux, 2023–24. The work covered:

- multi-source sensors across pharmaceutical utilities
- water rejection strategies, projected to save 10,000 m³ a year
- anomaly detection and forecasting
- semantic NLP for faults and maintenance
- natural-language interfaces to plant data on the floor

**PUBLIC, landmark (the belfry question):**

- **The belfry beside the Vieille Bourse is the Chambre de commerce belfry.** It stands on the Place du Théâtre, directly behind and facing the Vieille Bourse ("just behind the Grand'Place").
  - About 76 m tall (one source says 75 m), by Louis-Marie Cordonnier, built 1910–21, neo-Flemish.
  - It has a four-faced clock and a 25-bell carillon that plays "P'tit Quinquin" at noon. [CCI]
- **The UNESCO-listed belfry is a different tower.** It belongs to the Hôtel de Ville near the Porte de Paris, not on the Grand'Place.
  - 101 m, or 104 m including the lantern, by Émile Dubuisson, 1929–32, red brick and concrete.
  - Listed in 2005 together with other northern French belfries. [HdV]
- **The Vieille Bourse:** 1652–53, by Julien Destrée, Flemish mannerist.
  - Houses around a courtyard: 24 in the 1651 brief, 28 according to the current description. The sources conflict.
  - Four entrances and four galleries; caryatids, atlantes, garlands and cornucopias.
  - A gilded Mercury crowns the campanile.
  - Daily chess players and second-hand booksellers use the courtyard. [VB]
  - No source confirmed the materials of this building specifically. Sources describe Vieux-Lille in general as red brick with white stone.

**PUBLIC, work vocabulary and place:**

- **Saint-Amand-les-Eaux:** "les-Eaux" refers to its mineral waters, and the town's symbol is an 82 m Baroque abbey tower. [SAE]
- **The GSK site:** a vaccine site covering formulation, aseptic filling, lyophilisation and packaging. [GSK]
- **What a pharma water loop looks like:** [WFI]
  - polished 316L stainless pipes with orbital welds or tri-clamp joints
  - loops recirculating continuously, often held hot (e.g. 85 °C)
  - conductivity, TOC, flow, pressure and temperature sensors on each return
  - purified-water conductivity limit of ≤1.3 µS/cm

**PROPOSALS:**

- **A. "The belfry as a gauge" (recommended).**
  - Idea: the Vieille Bourse courtyard is ringed by polished pipe loops. Each house carries a sensor status light (multi-source sensors), and the belfry's four clock faces become gauges.
  - Discovery: tap a clock face and the hand sweeps to "10,000 m³/yr — projected saving, water rejection strategies". The word "projected" must stay.
  - Inspiration: [MM] for drawing the pipes as clean lines.
- **B. "The carillon hears the anomaly."**
  - Idea: the 25-bell carillon drum is drawn as a time series of pins, with one pin out of place.
  - Discovery: the bell rings off-key and the pin is highlighted, labelled "anomaly detection and forecasting".
  - The data is illustrative, so mark it that way.
- **C. "Ask the courtyard."**
  - Idea: the booksellers' stalls become a counter where a plant chart answers a question in plain words, standing for the natural-language interfaces on the floor.
  - Use a clearly labelled example question.
  - Needs from Lorenzo: which utilities and which interface he can show.
