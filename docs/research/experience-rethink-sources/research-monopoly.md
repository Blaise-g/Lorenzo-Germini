## Monopoly-style homepage board: research for Lorenzo's site

No repo files were changed. Scratch downloads (the Hasbro rules PDF, extracted text) are in /tmp/mono-research. **I did not open a browser.** None of the leads needed a rendered page, so there are no browser sessions or agent-browser daemon to clean up. ### 1. Board anatomy

**Spaces, US board** (position 0 = GO, moving clockwise; [en.wikipedia Monopoly](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monopoly_(game)>)):

- **Brown:** 1 Mediterranean, 2 Community Chest, 3 Baltic, 4 Income Tax, 5 Reading Railroad
- **Light blue:** 6 Oriental, 7 Chance, 8 Vermont, 9 Connecticut
- **Corner and pink:** 10 Jail/Just Visiting, 11 St. Charles, 12 Electric Company, 13 States, 14 Virginia, 15 Pennsylvania RR
- **Orange:** 16 St. James, 17 Community Chest, 18 Tennessee, 19 New York
- **Corner and red:** 20 Free Parking, 21 Kentucky, 22 Chance, 23 Indiana, 24 Illinois, 25 B&O RR
- **Yellow:** 26 Atlantic, 27 Ventnor, 28 Water Works, 29 Marvin Gardens
- **Corner and green:** 30 Go To Jail, 31 Pacific, 32 North Carolina, 33 Community Chest, 34 Pennsylvania Ave, 35 Short Line
- **Dark blue:** 36 Chance, 37 Park Place, 38 Luxury Tax, 39 Boardwalk

**Structure:**

- The 40 spaces are 22 streets in 8 colour groups, 4 railroads, 2 utilities, 3 Chance, 3 Community Chest, 2 taxes and 4 corners. The railroads sit mid-side at 5, 15, 25 and 35. The cheapest pair changed from purple to brown in 2008 (same source).
- Colour order runs brown, light blue, pink/fuchsia, orange, red, yellow, green, dark blue/purple. Source: the Italian list at [it.wikipedia Monopoly](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monopoly), which matches the US table.

**Title deed card** (verified in Hasbro's own rules sheet, C1009, [consumercare.hasbro.com](https://consumercare.hasbro.com/api/download/C1009_en-us); the copy served is the UK edition):

- The front has, in order:
  - a colour band headed "TITLE DEED" with the street name
  - "Rent", then "Rent with colour set", then rent with 1, 2, 3 and 4 houses and with a hotel (shown as icons)
  - "Houses cost … each" and "Hotels cost … each (plus 4 houses)"
  - "© 1935, 2021 HASBRO."
- The mortgage value is on the back. Station deeds have a different layout: "RENT", then "If 2/3/4 Stations are owned".

**Chance and Community Chest:** the board space tells you to "Take the top card from the matching deck and immediately do what it says. Return it to the bottom" (same PDF). Each deck has 16 cards. I did not get a primary source for the layout of the card faces themselves.

**Other space wording** (same PDF): GO says "COLLECT 200 SALARY AS YOU PASS". Free Parking: "Relax! Nothing happens."

**Italian names.** Source: [it.wikipedia](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monopoly), which is secondary. The Hasbro Italian rules URL returned the same English sheet.

- **Corners:** "VIA!", "Prigione / Transito", "Posteggio gratuito" (Hasbro calls it "Parcheggio Gratuito") and "In prigione!"
- **Cards:** "Imprevisti" (Chance) and "Probabilità" (Community Chest).
- **Taxes:** "Tassa Patrimoniale" and "Tassa di Lusso".
- **Stations:** Nord, Ovest, Est and Sud. In 1936 they were Marittima, Ferroviaria, Autobus and Aerea.
- **Utilities:** "Società Elettrica" and "Società Acqua Potabile" (Hasbro calls the second "Fontane").
- **Streets, cheapest to dearest:** Vicolo Corto and Vicolo Stretto, through Bastioni Gran Sasso, Via Accademia, Via Verdi, Via Marco Polo, Viale Costantino and Via Roma, to Viale dei Giardini and Parco della Vittoria.

### 2. History you can legitimately draw on

**The Landlord's Game** ([Google Patents US748626](https://patents.google.com/patent/US748626A/en)):

- Lizzie J. Magie filed on 23 March 1903, and US Patent 748,626 was granted on 5 January 1904. Google lists its expiry as 1921.
- Claim 1 describes "a game-board, having corner-spaces, one constituting the starting-point."
- Its own vocabulary:
  - "mother earth", the starting point. Each lap counts as labour, paying "wages, one hundred dollars".
  - "Public park": stop "without paying anything".
  - "No trespassing": go to jail until you throw a double or pay $50.
  - Others: "R. R.", "Franchises" (light, water), "Absolute necessities", "Luxury", "Legacy", "Poorhouse".
- A rules transcription is at [landlordsgame.info](https://landlordsgame.info/rules/lg-p04.html). "Lord Blueblood's Estate" does not appear in the 1904 text, so don't attribute it to the patent.

**From Landlord's Game to Monopoly** ([en.wikipedia Landlord's Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Landlord%27s_Game)):

- 1906: the Economic Game Co. edition.
- 1924: a second patent, no. 1,509,312. Its claims could not include the first patent's, which was by then public domain.
- 1935: Magie sold the 1924 patent to Parker Brothers for $500.
- The Ninth Circuit found that Monopoly developed between 1904 and 1934 among players, and that calling Darrow its inventor was "clearly erroneous" ([684 F.2d 1316](https://law.resource.org/pub/us/case/reporter/F2/684/684.F2d.1316.81-4281.html)).
- Parker Brothers began marketing Monopoly on 5 November 1935 ([en.wikipedia](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monopoly_(game)>)).
- Mary Pilon's _The Monopolists_ (2015) is the popular account ([Kirkus](https://www.kirkusreviews.com/book-reviews/mary-pilon/the-monopolists/)).

**Italy** ([it.wikipedia](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monopoly)):

- Editrice Giochi published "Monòpoli" in 1936. The Italianised name followed fascist-era rules against English names.
- Emilio Ceretti chose the street names, loosely inspired by Milan.
- The original names included "Via del Fascio", "Largo Littorio" and "Corso Impero"; the fascist ones were replaced after the war.
- Hasbro took over Italian distribution in 2009 and restored the name "Monopoly".

### 3. Legal line (cautious; not legal advice)

**What Hasbro claims.** Its notice, verbatim from [instructions.hasbro.com](https://instructions.hasbro.com/en-au/instruction/hasbro-gaming-road-trip-series-monopoly-game): "the Monopoly names and logos, the distinctive design of the gameboard, the four corner squares, the Mr. Monopoly name and character, as well as each of the distinctive elements of the board and playing pieces are trademarks of Hasbro." The deeds also carry "© 1935, 2021 HASBRO" (C1009 PDF).

**Registrations:**

- MONOPOLY word mark: US Reg. 0338834 (1936), as REPORTED by search results. I did not check it at the USPTO.
- An ABA Landslide article reportedly says Hasbro holds registrations for the board design, the railroad icon, and the Jail, Go to Jail and Free Parking artwork ([ABA](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/intellectual_property_law/resources/landslide/archive/not-playing-around-board-games-intellectual-property-law/)). The page returned 403, so this is REPORTED only.
- I could not verify whether "Chance", "Community Chest", "GO", "Free Parking" or "Title Deed" are registered as standalone word marks.

**Cases:**

- _Anti-Monopoly v. General Mills_ (9th Cir., 26 Aug 1982) held "Monopoly" generic for board games ([opinion](https://law.resource.org/pub/us/case/reporter/F2/684/684.F2d.1316.81-4281.html)).
- The Trademark Clarification Act of 1984 overturned the "purchaser motivation" test that ruling relied on ([ipmall](https://ipmall.info/sites/default/files/hosted_resources/lipa/trademarks/S.Rep.No.%2098-627,%2098th%20Cong.,%202d%20Sess.%20%28Sept.%2020%20%28legislative%20day,%20Sept.%2017%29,%201984%29.pdf)). The mark is live.
- Hasbro sued the maker of _Ghettopoly_ in 2003 and won a default judgment and an injunction in 2006 ([NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/id/wbna3339899); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghettopoly)).

**Copyright.** The US Copyright Office says "The idea for a game is not protected by copyright", and that the name and method of play aren't either. Rules text and board artwork may be protected ([copyright.gov](https://www.copyright.gov/register/tx-games.html)).

**Likely safe:**

- a loop of spaces with corners as anchors
- move, land and reveal mechanics
- colour-banded groups in your own palette
- data cards of your own design
- an accurate historical note on the Landlord's Game

The patents have expired, and mechanics aren't copyrightable.

**Risky:**

- the MONOPOLY or "Monopoli" name, or any "-opoly" coinage
- Mr. Monopoly
- the corner artwork (policeman, car, red GO arrow, jail cell)
- the logo and fonts
- reproducing the deed or card layouts and the "TITLE DEED" header
- the full combination of 40 spaces, the eight-colour sequence and the classic corner labels, which approaches the claimed "distinctive design of the gameboard"
- any suggestion of Hasbro endorsement

**Grey areas:** using the bare words "GO", "Chance", "Community Chest", "Free Parking" or "title deed" as labels. Using one in isolation is probably low-risk. Using all of them together is what builds the evocation.

**Where uncertainty remains:**

- All the sourcing above is US law. Italian and EU marks (EUIPO/UIBM: MONOPOLY, IMPREVISTI, PROBABILITÀ, VIA!) were **not checked**.
- A personal site that markets Lorenzo's professional services may not count as "non-commercial".

**Official and fan editions:**

- Winning Moves holds the licence for custom Hasbro editions worldwide outside North America ([paperjam.lu](https://en.paperjam.lu/article/luxembourg-themed-monopoly-fly)).
- A licensed **Monopoly Edizione Torino** launched in November 2025. Delta Pictures developed it under a Hasbro–Winning Moves licence, with Regione Piemonte and Banca Sella as partners ([mentelocale](https://www.mentelocale.it/torino/100869-monopoly-edizione-torino-il-nuovo-gioco-dedicato-alla-citt-e-ai-suoi-luoghi-simbolo.htm)).
- I found **no Cuneo edition**. The nearest is **Granda Land** (2019, Demoela Giochi), an independent Monopoly-like game set in the Provincia di Cuneo ([boardgamematcher](https://boardgamematcher.com/game/granda-land)).
- Hasbro's consumer "My Monopoly" personalisation was reportedly discontinued ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Monopoly)).

### 4. Digital and web precedents (all REPORTED; nothing OBSERVED)

- **Monopoly Plus** (Ubisoft/Asobo, 2014):
  - REPORTED: the store listing says a 3D city at the centre "lives and evolves as you play" ([Xbox](https://xbox.com/en-US/games/store/1/C0CB2CVZVSNR)).
  - REPORTED, from Pure Xbox's 2014 review ([review](https://www.purexbox.com/reviews/xbox-one/monopoly_plus)):
    - properties are visibly redeveloped as they're bought
    - on the classic board, the camera "swings around to show the most relevant section"
    - some animations are unskippable, which slows play
  - Sound: no source covered it.
- **Monopoly Madness** (Ubisoft, Dec 2021) is a real-time street race, not an animated board ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/monopoly-madness/)).
- **Ubisoft's newer Monopoly:** REPORTED as having a 3D city with a day/night and weather cycle ([TheXboxHub](https://www.thexboxhub.com/ubisoft-brings-monopoly-to-life-with-enhanced-visuals-and-gameplay/)).
- **Monopoly City Streets** (Hasbro, 2009): REPORTED to have used Google Maps as the board and buckled under launch demand ([ABC](https://abcnews.go.com/Technology/monopoly-city-streets-launches-google/story?id=8525472)).
- **Web portfolios:** I found no live portfolio that uses a Monopoly board as navigation. What exists:
  - a classic board built in CSS Grid on CodePen ([johnnycopes](https://codepen.io/johnnycopes/pen/yzQyMp)); I didn't open it
  - a Monopoly-styled CV by Inès Es-Sbai ([Planète Grandes Écoles](https://www.planetegrandesecoles.com/?p=13770))

### Implications for Lorenzo's board

- **Borrow the structure, not the skin.** A loop of spaces with four corner anchors, mid-side "stations" and colour-banded groups is free to use. Use your own palette and don't reproduce the eight-colour sequence in order.
- **Don't copy the 40-space count and the corner labels together.** Fewer spaces (Cuneo, three cities, roles, 4 projects, a few notes) fits the content and moves the design away from Hasbro's claimed board design.
- **Cuneo as the start:** label it in Lorenzo's own words (e.g. "Partenza · Cuneo"), or use Magie's public-domain "Mother Earth" idea that each lap earns "wages", rather than "GO" or "VIA!".
- **Free Parking becomes "Public Park"** (patent 748,626 says you stop there "without paying anything"). It's a fitting, citable home for About and contact.
- **Rename the cards.** "Field notes" instead of Community Chest/Probabilità, and "?" or "Next" instead of Chance/Imprevisti. Keep the draw-and-reveal gesture, not the words.
- **Title deeds become role cards** in an original layout: a colour band with the company, then dates, then documented results ranked like rent tiers. Avoid the "TITLE DEED" header and Hasbro's typography and icons.
- **Side projects as "lines" or railroads.** Generic stations are fine. Don't use Stazione Nord/Sud/Est/Ovest, or the 1936 Marittima/Aerea names if they read as a copy.
- **Drop Jail entirely.** Never use Mr. Monopoly, the policeman, car or GO-arrow art, any "-opoly" or "Monopoli" name, or "Get Out of Jail Free".
- **Cite the Landlord's Game.** A footer line like "Board loop after Lizzie Magie's The Landlord's Game (US Patent 748,626, 1904)" is accurate and public domain. Avoid "official", "edition" or Hasbro wording, since the licensed Torino edition exists.
- **Motion lessons from Monopoly Plus:** the camera should frame the active space, the city can visibly "develop" as roles are revealed, and every animation must be skippable or respect reduced motion.
- **Sound:** no precedent evidence either way. Keep the site silent by default.
- **Granda Land (Cuneo, 2019) is a local precedent** for a Cuneo-themed property loop. Worth a nod, not a model.

**Could not verify:**

- whether GO, Chance, Community Chest, Free Parking or Title Deed are standalone registered marks
- EU and Italian trademark status
- a Hasbro primary source for the Italian names
- the visual layout of the Chance and Community Chest card faces
- the content of the ABA article (403)
- Ubisoft's sound design
